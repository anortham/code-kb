## Round-4 corpus review against r3: fixes

The round-4 corpus delta against `target/release-corpus-r3` found these defects. All are fixed and tested (516 tests pass):

- **TS relative imports (`pending_target_predicate`):**
  - The rule took the last dotted segment of `../core/util.js`, which is `js`. It also treated `importedName` as a module, so `util` matched every `util.*` file.
  - This swapped the zod callers of the v3 and v4 `joinValues`.
  - Fix: `is_relative_import_path` and `relative_import_matches` build the path tail without the extension. The owner route also has to agree with a relative import.
  - Test: `a_relative_typescript_import_with_a_js_extension_picks_the_file_it_names`.
- **Ambiguity:** `outer_definitions` drops an export row that repeats a definition on the same line, and drops function locals. zod `refs parse` now resolves.
- **Lookup fold:** "1+ import" is replaced. The text says "at least N" only when the cut is inside the exact-name rows.
- **Skeleton "defines":**
  - It skips non-test C/C++ functions nested in functions. These are macro misparses such as `JSON_CATCH` and `if`.
  - It removes repeated names.
- **Display:** `qualify_members` no longer shows `Cart.Cart.empty`.
- **Not defects:**
  - doctest declares `std::string` inside `namespace std`.
  - The flask search changes are score decimals only.
- **Left for the user:** jq `jv_dump_term`, where an `#ifdef` splits braces. tree-sitter swallows the next functions. This is a julie limit and was in 3.6.0.
- **Timing:** refs and blast on hermes-agent and zod are the same as HEAD (built in `scratchpad/head-target`).
