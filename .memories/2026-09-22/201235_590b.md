## Implemented truthful blast-radius limits

### What
Added output truncation and discovery ceiling metadata to `BlastRadiusResult`, and made CLI/MCP blast-radius output truthful for zero limits and bounded discovery.

### Why
The previous implementation treated `limit: 0` as 20 and inferred traversal saturation at exactly 200 rows, which could overstate or hide results.

### How
`compute_blast_radius_scoped` probes 201 walk rows and 11 deterministic stem-matched paths without consuming probe rows. Formatting distinguishes returned rows, requested limit truncation, traversal and file discovery ceilings, and compact text caps. Focused regressions cover exact one-row and two-row limits, zero output, test-only saturation, 200/201 traversal rows, empty capped discovery, CLI JSON, and MCP text.
