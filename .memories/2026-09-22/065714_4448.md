## Lead review of the v1.6.0 wave: one fix, the skeleton event marker

**WHAT:** Reviewed the five wave commits (c32f501 pin 3.3.0, 23c7485 property alias, 12a540c Qt C++ tests, c7d1f7d docs and claim, ec02099 version bump). **HOW:** Keyed on the row kind, not on language or Qt metadata; QML `signal x()` rows are unchanged, so the v1.5.0 measured skeletons keep their text.
