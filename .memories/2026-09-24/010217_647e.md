## Release stability gate: real-project corpus check

**WHY:** v2.0.0 through v2.1.0 needed quick follow-up releases. Issues #2 and #4 came from real C# files, and #3 came from real harness startup. Every preflight step tested fixtures only, and nothing compared the new build with the last release on real code.

**WHAT:**
- `scripts/release-corpus-check.py` and `scripts/release-corpus.txt` are new. The corpus has 14 local repos (Rust, Swift, Go, JS, TS, Python, Java, Kotlin, C#, Razor, C, C++, Ruby) plus the julie-extractors `fixtures` at `main` for the other languages. Each entry is exported with `git archive`, and each side scans into its own `--db`.
- The script fails on: a failed scan, a symbol loss over 0.5%, a scan more than 1.5x slower (3 s floor), an old index that the new build does not rebuild (upgrade path), or a tool call that worked before and now errors or returns nothing.
- It reports samples of gained and lost call edges with their source lines, and diffs of changed tool outputs. A person reviews these.
- `release-preflight.sh` step 9/9 requires `target/release-corpus/report.md` with PASS, `new: code-kb <ver>, julie-extract <pin>`, and a time newer than the HEAD commit.
- `docs/RELEASING.md` has new manual checks 6 (corpus) and 7 (harness dogfood in Claude Code and Codex on a non-Rust worktree), and a clean-launcher install smoke in section 5.
- The v2.1.0 baseline archive is at `~/.code-kb/search-eval/bin/v2.1.0`.

**Found by the first run:** my `reference_name` cut long caller names (CSS selectors) to `….navigation`. I renamed it `callee_name` and limited it to callee rows that hold `(`. Go doc snippets joined with `//` markers, so `match_line` now strips the markers on continuation lines.

**IMPACT:** The full corpus run takes about 6.5 min. It was PASS on the uncommitted code-kb fixes with julie 3.5.0. The upgrade check only exercises a rebuild once julie-extract reports a new version.
