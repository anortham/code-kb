## WHAT
Evaluated code-kb at 54f2c08 and compared io, Phoebe, Miller, and Julie. Saved docs/reviews/2026-09-26-project-evaluation.md and 2026-09-26-evaluation-data.json.

## WHY
The owner asked for five improvements and an unbiased assessment of token/context savings. Existing full-file estimates do not establish successful-task savings or fewer compactions.

## HOW
Audited telemetry source, existing experiments, and release evidence. Ran retrieval_score.py on its pinned corpus and ten live output probes with cl100k_base. Skeletons were 61.4–90.1% smaller than their full files; queries.rs still returned 14,541 tokens. Four bodies were 2.4–11.2% larger than exact source spans, which assume the location is already known. Telemetry uses bytes/4, repeated full-file baselines, and per-call clamping; it contains no compaction comparator. Before evaluation, v2.2.2 had 90 calls, 70,539 served estimates, and 4,373,600 full-file upper-bound savings with coverage 83/90. These totals have different coverage and must not be divided into an efficiency ratio.

## IMPACT
Recommendations, not approved implementation: measure complete tasks; improve relationship correctness and slow queries; bound large output; extend held-out correctness gates; trial separate file/content retrieval. Preserve AST/SQLite and avoid adopting io's embedding runtime on current evidence. Existing Sept23 retrieval-value backlog covers much of measurement/routing work. No paid model replays were run.
