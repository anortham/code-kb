## Serialize concurrent index rebuilds

**WHAT:** `ensure_index_matches_extractor` in `crates/code-kb-core/src/sync.rs` takes an exclusive lock (`std::fs::File::lock`) on `<db>.rebuild.lock` before it checks and rebuilds. New test `test_servers_starting_together_on_a_stale_index_rebuild_it_once` in `freshness_test.rs`.

**WHY:** In the benchmark, 8 `code-kb serve` processes started on aspnetcore within 1 minute. Its index was written by julie 3.6.2. Each one began its own rebuild into the same `artifact.db.rebuild` and deleted the other servers' side files. The rebuild took about 2.75 min (23:47:17 to 23:50:03 UTC). During that time every tool call waited 5 s and answered "Indexing ... started".

**HOW:** The first server rebuilds. The others wait on the lock, then re-check and find the index current. If the lock cannot be taken, the server logs a warning and continues without the lock. Without the lock, the test fails: one thread's rebuild returns an error.

**IMPACT:** One rebuild, not N. CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md invariant 7 updated.
