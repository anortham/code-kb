## WHAT
Verified the two-index receiver-query fix at source `0551d318`. Wrote `docs/reviews/2026-09-27-reference-query-latency.md` and its data JSON, and linked the earlier report to the follow-up.

## WHY
The owner rejected a 20-second warm reference query. Two missing lookup indexes caused repeated global variable and same-name call-site scans; aggregation and extra symbol indexes were measured and rejected.

## HOW
Five alternating pairs on frozen Flask/Hermes copies produced 50 successful requests with identical normalized results across versions. Hermes references fell from 20.08 to 2.04 s median, CPU time from 20.04 to 2.03 s, cached-read traffic from 40.07 GiB to 315.10 MiB. Physical storage reads stayed zero. Impact fell 8.17 to 6.15 s. Net Hermes database growth was 35.125 MiB; allocated index space 37.10 MiB reused free pages. The release initializer upgraded a fresh copy in 0.780 s and rechecked it in 0.08 ms. Retained Hermes RSS was 26.69 versus 26.91 MiB.

Linux 546 Rust tests, lint, format, and release build pass; Windows 15 database +136 affected integration +2 protocol tests pass. New guard detects excessive SQLite steps without wall-clock assertions. Read-only final review found only a CPU percentage rounding correction, now fixed. Work stays local; no push/release.
