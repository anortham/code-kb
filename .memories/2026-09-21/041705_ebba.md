## Plan 023 task 1: stop words, tie order, and the member whole-name bonus

**WHAT**: Three rerank rules in `crates/code-kb-core/src/queries.rs`, with unit tests, two corpus cases, and the docs.

- `STOP_WORDS` grew from 23 to 116 entries and is now sorted alphabetically. The 93 new words are English function words (`was`, `whether`, `another`, `how`, `so`, ...). The directional words `up down out over under back into onto` stay content words because they live inside identifiers. The three users (`sanitize_fts5_query`, `trigram_name_terms`, `rerank_words`) are unchanged, so a stop word is still dropped only when a content word remains.
- The final `sort_by` in `rerank_with` gained one step between `name_strength` and the BM25 comparisons: a name that starts with `_` sorts after a name that does not.
- `name_bonus` gives `W_NAME_WHOLE` (100) only when `DEFINITION_KINDS` holds `normalize_kind(&symbol.kind)`. Every other kind gets `W_NAME_ALL_WORDS` (60) for a whole-name match. `name_tier` still reports `whole`.

**WHY**: The offline simulation in plan 023 found three defects. Function words earned term credit because the rarity weight is computed over code text. A private twin beat its public entry point on BM25. A constant or enum member named exactly the query beat a function that held the word plus context.

**HOW**: Unit tests in `queries::tests`:

- `stop_words_cover_english_function_words_but_not_identifier_directions`
- `a_public_name_sorts_before_its_private_twin_at_an_equal_score`
- `a_whole_name_constant_yields_to_a_function_that_holds_the_word_with_context`

Corpus cases in `crates/code-kb-core/tests/search_corpus_test.rs`:

- `a_function_holding_the_word_with_context_beats_a_member_named_exactly_the_query` uses the existing `RenderMode::Outline` enum member against `render_codebase_outline` on the query `outline`.
- `a_public_name_ranks_before_its_private_twin` needed a new fixture file, `tools/skills.py`, with `create_skill` and `_create_skill`. The private twin has the shorter docstring, so it wins the BM25 tie-break without rule 2.

Both corpus cases were checked against a temporary revert of the two rules and failed, so they measure the rules and not the fixture.

**IMPACT**: CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md invariant 6 now state the tie order and the two bonus values. The files stay byte-identical.
