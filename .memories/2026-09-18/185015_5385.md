## Plan 018 fixes landed on branch fix/julie-data-usage

**WHAT:** Seven commits on top of main implement every finding in `docs/plans/018-julie-data-usage-review.md`:
- `find_related_tests` reads pending call edges through the shared `pending_target_predicate`, so tests in other files appear in `get_symbol_context`. Documentation rows (`content_type = 'documentation'`) are excluded from related tests and blast-radius test lists.
- Local variables and parameters (a `variable` under a function, method, or constructor) stay out of the FTS index, `lookup_symbol`, and `search_symbols` unless `kind = "variable"`.
