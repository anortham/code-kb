## Phase 3: Agent Output Quality (Items 10-18)

- **Item 10 (Signatures & body_hash):** Added metadata header `// path:start-end (name) body_hash=<hash>` followed by signature and body in `format_symbol_body` and `format_context_slice`. Documented `expected_body_hash` in tool schemas. Standardized on SHA256.
- **Item 11 (Blast Radius non-code seeds):** Filtered out imports, variables, properties, modules from blast radius seeds, joins, and results. Capped depth at 5 in `blast_radius_op`.
- **Item 12 (Qualified find_references):** Resolved qualified symbol names via `get_symbol_by_name`, isolated callers to single symbol ID, validated direction (`callers|callees`), and added limit reached footer.
- **Item 13 (Codebase Outline tags):** Filtered file tags to primary definition kinds (`function`, `struct`, `enum`, `trait`, etc.) with `parent_symbol_id IS NULL`, skipping zero-symbol files.
- **Item 14 (File Skeleton noise):** Sanitized signatures cutting at `{` before showing hidden lines; capped doc comments at 3 lines.
- **Item 15 (Path relativization):** Added `Workspace::relativize_filter` to normalize absolute paths, URIs, and relative prefixes across outline, symbol, search, and blast-radius.
- **Item 16 (Error paths):** Removed suffix fallback from `get_file`; added `FileNotFound` and `IsADirectory` in `ops.rs`; added up to 3 near-match suggestions on `SymbolNotFound`.
- **Item 17 (find_symbol normalization):** Added `normalize_kind` mapping; direct resolution of qualified names; labeled FTS fallback.
- **Item 18 (Telemetry & logging on hot path):** Created `open_telemetry_db` and `record_tool_call_conn` to reuse a persistent connection with `synchronous=NORMAL` and prune logs older than 30 days. Rolling logs capped at 7 files with `max_log_files(7)`; stderr tracing enabled only with `--verbose` or `RUST_LOG`.
- **Item 21 (Formatters consolidation):** Shared `format_structural_facts`, `format_replace_symbol_result`, `format_symbol_body`, and `format_fact_categories` across CLI and MCP.
