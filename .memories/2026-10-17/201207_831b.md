## malloc_trim after each tool call, and an excluded-precision gate mark

**WHAT**
- `Cargo.toml`: `unsafe_code` lint `forbid` → `deny`.
- `crates/code-kb-cli/src/mcp/server.rs`: `release_freed_memory()` runs after `handle_call_tool` in the `tools/call` branch. On Linux glibc it calls `malloc_trim(0)` (the only unsafe code, `#[allow(unsafe_code)]`); other targets get an empty fn.
- `scripts/reach_gate.py`: new mark `excluded_precision` 0.98 = excluded sites that cover no truth line / all excluded sites. A language with no excluded sites passes and shows `n/a`. `disagreements` now lists excluded sites that cover a truth line. Tests in `scripts/test_reach_gate.py` (63 pass).
- Docs: CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md memory rule and gate marks, RELEASING.md, evals/reach/README.md, review.

**WHY**
- The owner approved both on 2026-10-17 ("1. allow the call. 2. yes").
- serve kept 69–88 MB of freed heap after a large callers report.

**EVIDENCE**
- RSS after the interleaved benchmark: aspnetcore 88.2 → 58.5 MB, hermes-agent 113.7 → 51.4 MB (v2.6.0: 38.3, 31.6). Latency unchanged (get p50 3,242 ms).
- The work ran as a 4-agent Sonnet workflow (2 implementers, 2 adversarial reviewers, both reviews ok); the lead checked the diffs and ran the checks.

**IMPACT**
- The remaining ~20 MB over v2.6.0 is fragmented heap that malloc_trim cannot return.
